Med-2D SegNet: A Light Weight Deep Neural Network for Medical 2D Image Segmentation

Noyon Kumar Sarkar

arXiv preprint, 2025

Med-2D SegNet is an efficient and accurate architecture for medical image segmentation. Built around the compact Med Block, it reaches state-of-the-art performance with only 2.07M parameters and demonstrates strong cross-dataset generalization across KVASIR-SEG, PH2, EndoVis and GLAS, in both binary and multi-class segmentation — balancing accuracy against the compute budgets available in resource-constrained clinical settings.

Design of a 6-bit Threshold Inverter Quantization (TIQ) Flash Analog to Digital Converter

Noyon Kumar Sarkar

arXiv preprint, 2025 — M.S. thesis research

A 6-bit flash ADC built entirely from CMOS inverters acting as comparators, eliminating the resistor ladder and with it the sensitivity to fabrication and temperature variation that conventional flash architectures carry. Designed in 0.25 µm CMOS technology and simulated in Siemens Tanner EDA.

Brain Controlled Wheelchair with Smart Features

Noyon Kumar Sarkar

arXiv preprint, 2025 — undergraduate thesis research

An Arduino-based wheelchair controlled by the user's brain waves, acquired with a NeuroSky MindWave 2 sensor and relayed through a companion Android application, with additional assistive features for independent operation.
