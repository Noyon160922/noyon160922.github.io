Research

Soft robots trade rigid links and motors for materials that deform. That shift moves the hard problems into the material and the circuit — how to get large motion from low voltage, and how to build electronics that stretch with the body they control. My work sits on that boundary.

Current Work — ECMS Lab, Gachon University

My current work spans the full stack of a soft robot: I design and build the actuators that give it motion, the electronic circuits that drive and control it, and I bring the two together into a complete working robot.

Actuation

Soft actuators

I design and fabricate the soft actuators that give the robot its motion — the "muscle" layer of the system, built to deform predictably and repeatably under electrical drive.

Circuits

Driving and control electronics

An actuator is only half a robot. I design the electronic circuits that drive and control it — the layer that turns a soft material into something that responds to a command. This draws directly on my background in analog and mixed-signal circuit design.

Systems

Complete soft robot

Integrating actuators, circuits and power into one working robot — the point where a material and a circuit stop being separate components and start being a machine that moves on command.

Earlier Research

Mixed-signal design

6-bit threshold inverter quantization (TIQ) flash ADC

My postgraduate research project at Khulna University. I designed a flash analog-to-digital converter using only CMOS inverters as comparators, removing resistor ladders and their sensitivity to process and temperature variation. Implemented in 0.25 µm CMOS and simulated in Siemens Tanner EDA.

Assistive robotics

Brain-controlled wheelchair with smart features

My undergraduate thesis: an Arduino-based wheelchair driven by EEG signals captured with a NeuroSky MindWave sensor and relayed through an Android application. An early lesson in building machines that respond to a human body rather than to a joystick.

Deep learning

Med-2D SegNet — efficient medical image segmentation

A compact segmentation network built around the Med Block, reaching state-of-the-art accuracy with 2.07M parameters and strong cross-dataset generalization across KVASIR-SEG, PH2, EndoVis and GLAS — aimed at diagnostic tools that must run in resource-constrained settings.

Applied Machine Learning

Before starting at Gachon I worked for two years as a machine learning engineer, which is where I learned to build systems that have to keep working outside a notebook.

Handwritten Bangla OCR

An end-to-end recognition system for handwritten Bangla at word and sentence level, using a CRNN with a ResNet-34 backbone. Bangla is an under-resourced script with little annotated handwriting data, so I combined semi-supervised training — self-training and consistency regularization — with synthetic text generated from custom in-house Bangla handwriting fonts, cutting the manual annotation burden substantially.

Document OCR and computer vision in production

Table extraction from supply-chain and government documentation (TIN, BIN, tax return forms) and HR systems, served through APIs to multiple client organizations, with automated data processing and retraining pipelines. Also built a face recognition pipeline (MTCNN detection, ArcFace recognition) over image, video and live webcam streams.
